    HOW TO PLAY

    Type a single word and press ENTER to drop it into the room. Add more words to build a bouncing pile. Grab a word with your mouse or finger, drag it around, and release while moving to throw it. Words tumble, rotate and collide. Hold a word to explore its definition and history. CLEAR removes the pile.

    ARCHIVES // READING & RESEARCH

    SOURCES

    Add files from your devices' storage. Click "recognize image text" to isolate text in images, PDFSs, books, etc. Double-click to select and drag a word. The definitions and etemology can be seen on the bottom of the word box.
